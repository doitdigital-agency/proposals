07The growth system

Always-on content builds visibility. Quarterly campaigns create intent.

Layer 1

Always-on executive content

Three posts per week.

  • Trust. Make Alexi familiar, human and worth following.
  • Authority. Show how Alexi thinks and what Bonfire knows.
  • Conversion. Give the right person a clear reason to start a conversation.
Layer 2

Bonfire events as proof assets

The room is already filling. We use the event before, during and after it to create:

  • Market insight
  • Speaker perspective
  • Audience questions
  • Strategic takeaways
  • Evidence of Bonfire's access and expertise
Layer 3

One quarterly executive briefing

Rather than forcing a monthly webinar schedule, we run one focused briefing every 90 days around a high-value issue for larger consumer brands.

Consumer Brand Growth Briefing: What Marketing Leaders Should Measure Now

The briefing is not designed simply to fill a room. It is designed to identify the right people in the room and create a reason for the next conversation.

Layer 4

Retargeting, later

Once the organic audience and campaign data are large enough, LinkedIn retargeting can reach:

  • Video viewers
  • Post engagers
  • Profile visitors
  • Event attendees
  • Briefing registrants
  • People from target consumer-brand accounts

Paid media is the amplification layer. It is not the strategy.

09The content mechanism

How 45 minutes a fortnight becomes strategic content and commercial conversations.

1

Insight extraction

Two recorded 45-minute interviews each month. We listen to Alexi's actual answers, examples and phrasing rather than producing generic posts from a transcript.

OutputTwo to three weeks of raw material per interview
2

Strategic positioning

We audit the current presence, research the ideal buyers and position Alexi around one clear category.

OutputPositioning brief and profile live by week two
3

Voice-matched writing

We turn the interviews into twelve posts across Trust, Authority and Conversion.

OutputTwelve drafts prepared five business days before publishing
4

Editorial production

We maintain the calendar, voice profile and content bank. Every draft is checked against the agreed quality standard before Alexi sees it.

OutputA calendar Alexi can review in five minutes
5

Approval and publishing

Alexi approves or edits each post. One revision round is included. Nothing publishes without sign-off.

OutputThree posts per week, published on schedule
6

Commercial tracking

Relevant conversations, registrations, calls and opportunities are logged.

OutputMonthly review in commercial terms
